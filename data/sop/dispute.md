# Payment / EMI Dispute — Collection SOP

## Trigger
Customer says the amount, due date, payment status, or account information is incorrect.

## Agent Actions
1. Stop making unsupported claims about the amount.
2. Identify the disputed field.
3. Retrieve account/payment information through an approved tool.
4. If the discrepancy remains, create an escalation case.
5. Provide the customer with the next expected action or callback process.

## Allowed Outcome
`DISPUTE`, `PAYMENT_DISPUTE`, or `ESCALATE`.

## Prohibited
Do not argue with the customer or invent account information.
