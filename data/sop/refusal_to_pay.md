# Refusal to Pay — Collection SOP

## Trigger
Customer refuses to make the requested payment (e.g., "I won't pay", "ill wont pay", "నేను కట్టను", "కట్టేది లేదు").

## Agent Actions
1. Maintain a calm, respectful, empathetic, and professional tone in accordance with RBI Fair Debt Collection Guidelines.
2. Politely explain the financial consequences of continued non-payment:
   - **Late Payment Penalties**: Penal charges and overdue interest continue to accrue as per contract.
   - **Credit Bureau Impact**: Overdue status is reported to credit agencies (CIBIL/Experian), reducing their credit score.
   - **Future Loan Eligibility**: A diminished credit score severely restricts their eligibility for future personal loans, home loans, vehicle loans, and credit cards from any financial institution.
3. Encourage the customer to make at least a partial payment or commit to a specific date to protect their credit standing.
4. If the refusal is due to financial distress or unemployment, offer to register a hardship review.
5. Multilingual Support: If the customer communicates in Telugu, provide the explanation and counseling in fluent, respectful Telugu.

## Allowed Outcome
`REFUSAL_TO_PAY`, `HARDSHIP`, `DISPUTE`, `WILL_PAY`, or `ESCALATE`.

## Prohibited
- Threatening police action, physical seizure, or unlawful harassment (strictly prohibited by RBI).
- Promising unauthorized fee waivers or debt write-offs.
