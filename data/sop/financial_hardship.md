# Financial Hardship — Collection SOP

## Trigger
Customer indicates inability to pay because of financial hardship.

## Agent Actions
1. Acknowledge the customer's situation.
2. Ask only the information required for hardship assessment.
3. Explain that assistance is subject to eligibility and approval.
4. Retrieve approved hardship options if available.
5. If a formal arrangement is requested, create/escalate the case.
6. Record the customer's preferred callback date when applicable.

## Allowed Outcome
`HARDSHIP`, `CALLBACK_REQUESTED`, or `ESCALATE_HARDSHIP`.

## Prohibited
Never guarantee restructuring, settlement, waiver, reduced EMI, or extension.
