          Samvaad — Regulatory & Agent Rules
        

        
      


      
      

        
        
        
      


      

        
        

          

            
1. Golden Architecture Rule (Zero Financial Hallucination)

            
Financial facts (outstanding balance, EMI amount, due date, payment status) must be retrieved strictly from authoritative core database tools. RAG is only used for policy interpretation. The agent must never guess or invent numbers.

          

          

            
2. Payment Confirmation Protocol (Already Paid SOP)

            
Verification tools execute before response generation. If the UTR / transaction is verified with status 'SUCCESS', the agent must immediately confirm full clearance without asking the customer to wait or hold.

          

          

            
3. Telephony Voice Brevity

            
All spoken responses are limited to 1 to 2 concise sentences (under 35 words) to ensure real-time latency and natural phone conversation pacing.

          

        


        
        

          

            
1. Permissible Calling Hours

            
Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Fair Recovery Code, customer communications are permitted strictly between 08:00 AM and 07:00 PM local time.

          

          

            
2. Anti-Harassment & Civil Conduct

            
Strict prohibition against abusive language, intimidation, threatening police or legal force without court decree, or public humiliation.

          

          

            
3. Privacy & Authorized Communication

            
Debt recovery details cannot be disclosed to unauthorized third parties, co-workers, or relatives without written consent.

          

        


        
        

          

            
• Already Paid SOP

            
Extract transaction reference or UTR. Verify via verify_payment_transaction. If verified, update core ledger and confirm zero overdue balance.

          

          

            
• Financial Hardship SOP

            
Acknowledge job loss or medical emergency empathetically. Register hardship ticket via register_hardship_request. Never guarantee unauthorized debt write-offs.

          

          

            
• Payment Dispute SOP

            
Open formal dispute ticket via create_escalation_ticket. Pause automated escalation and assign to accounts reconciliation team.
